/// ABOUT

WESTCOAST
RIPPERS

Based out of Greymouth on the West Coast of New Zealand, Westcoast Rippers is about getting out and having a crack at the rough stuff. 4WD tracks, river crossings, gravel roads, mud — the kind of country that doesn't care what you drive as long as you know what you're doing.

The channel documents real West Coast adventures: bar crossings, vehicle builds, track exploration, and everything in between. No scripts, no set-ups — just what happens when you point the wheels somewhere interesting and hit record.

The West Coast is one of New Zealand's most unforgiving and beautiful places. Rain, rivers, mud and mountains. That's the backdrop. That's the point.
